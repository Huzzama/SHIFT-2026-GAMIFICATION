# 4. Journey

Answers **where am I going?** It is the course as a route, not as a list.

## Recalculation strip

At the top, when your route drifted: *"Recalculating your route… Nothing was lost: the route below reorders itself around where you actually are."*

It is the GPS metaphor and it is literal: the route reorders, you are not scolded.

## The route

Every activity is a stop, grouped by module. The state shows in the dot on the left:

| State | How it looks | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Completed | Green dot | Submitted |
| You are here | Mint dot with a halo | Your current position |
| Missed | Hollow amber dot | Overdue and still open — **never in red** |
| Upcoming | Grey dot | Not yet |
| Locked | Dimmed text | Opens later |

The stops that close a module are **checkpoints**: the dot becomes square and the label appears. They are the "you made it this far" moments.

Each stop carries its minutes and its date in plain language: *due today*, *due in 3 days*, *was due 2 days ago*. Never a count of how many you owe.

## The rest of this screen

- **Header** with the percentage and, if you wrote it, your destination.
- **Next Best Step**, the same one as in Home — it is the same data, not a copy.
- **Your way back**, only if the route is recalculated: three short sessions.
- **One social line**, discreet: how many people worked on this module this week. Opens Community. It is one line on purpose — Journey is not a feed.
