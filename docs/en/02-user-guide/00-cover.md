## How to read this book

This guide describes FARO as it is built today: six tabs, two contextual screens and a Canvas integration that can run in two modes. Each chapter covers one screen: what it shows, what every control does, and **the exact rule that decides what you see**. When a rule has a number (a threshold, a score, a percentage), the number is the one in the code.

If you only have five minutes, read chapter 1 and chapter 5. They are the reason FARO exists.

## The two modes

FARO can run on test data or on your real Canvas. The difference changes no screen; it changes where the data comes from.

| Mode | What you see | How to recognise it |
|---|---|---|
| **Simulated** | A sample course (*Gestión de Proyectos*, 6 modules, 16 activities) with a student who has not logged in for 5 days. | In **Profile → Canvas** the badge says *Simulated*. |
| **Live** | Your real Canvas course, your modules, your submissions. | The badge says *Live* and your course name appears. |

In *Live* mode FARO never talks to Canvas directly: it goes through its own server, which holds the credential. That is explained in Book 1 (Security) and set up by following Book 3 (Technical documentation).
