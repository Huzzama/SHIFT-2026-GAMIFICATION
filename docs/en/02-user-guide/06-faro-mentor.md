# 6. FARO Mentor

The **FARO** tab. A chat, but not a generic chatbot: it only knows about your course and is built to guide you, not to do the work for you.

## How to use it

It opens with a message that already knows your situation — different if you are back from an absence than if you are on track. Below every reply there are **follow-up chips**: tapping them sends that question without typing it. It is the fastest way to use it.

Type freely or use the chips. It understands questions about: what to do now, being behind, having little time, not understanding something, not feeling like it, and planning the week.

## The five styles

At the top, as chips: **Direct**, **Encouraging**, **Detailed**, **Friendly**, **Challenge me**.

They change how it talks to you, never what it can do. "Challenge me" will not give you answers that "Encouraging" refuses; it just sounds different. The academic and safety rules are the same across all five.

## What it knows about you — exactly

This is the complete list, and it is built in a single file of the code:

- The course name
- Your progress percentage
- Your next activity and how long it takes
- Your goal and your destination sentence
- The minutes you said you have
- Your momentum and your internal friction state
- The style and language you chose

**What it never receives:** your name, your email, your photo, your bio, your Canvas identifiers, your grades, your history of previous conversations. If a piece of data is not built in that file, the mentor never sees it. It is not a written policy: it is the only way it can receive data.

## What it will not do

If you ask it to solve graded work, it refuses — and offers to break it into steps, explain the concept or review your reasoning. That refusal is the same across the five styles.

At the bottom of the screen is the note that says so without small print.
