# 8. Rewards and Progress

FARO gives points for **persisting**, not for clicking. Everything that awards points requires something real to have happened: an activity finished, a module closed, a return after disconnecting, an answer that helped someone.

## How points are earned

| Action | Points | Exact rule |
|---|---|---|
| Complete an activity | **10** | A study session recorded on a milestone. |
| Close a module | **50** | Paid once, when the last milestone of the module is completed. |
| Rhythm bonus | **+5** | Every 3 consecutive days with activity. What was earned is not lost when the streak breaks: it just stops adding. |
| Comeback bonus | **+25** | Paid once when you return after 3 or more days without activity (the friction threshold) and complete something. |
| Answer marked helpful | **+5** | Another student marked your answer as helpful in Community. |
| Finish a study room | **+20** | Joining the room gives no points. Finishing the full session does. |

**Redemption:** 100 points = 1 reward unit. The Rewards screen shows the breakdown by origin — course, rhythm, comeback, community — so where every point came from can always be audited.

## Resilience achievements

There are five. None is earned by opening the app.

| Achievement | Earned when |
|---|---|
| **The Comeback** | You return after 3 or more days without activity and complete an activity. |
| **Back on Track** | You have at least 2 recorded sessions and your momentum is back to 65% or more. |
| **Weathered the Storm** | You complete a session while having 2 or more overdue activities when you started it. |
| **Smart Session** | You complete a session that fitted in the time you declared you had. |
| **Finisher** | Your progress reaches 100%. |

While an achievement is not earned, FARO shows a **hint** of what it would take. Never a reproach.

## Community recognitions

| Recognition | Earned when |
|---|---|
| **Community Builder** | You take part in the course space consistently. |
| **Knowledge Sharer** | You share resources or notes that others use. |
| **Helpful Peer** | Your answers accumulate helpful marks. |
| **Study Companion** | You complete study rooms alongside others. |
| **Community Comeback** | You come back to the community after a period away. |

## Progress

The progress percentage is **not invented by FARO**: it comes from Canvas. It is activities with a recorded submission ÷ total course activities. An overdue date does not count as "done", and an activity that belongs to no module does not enter the count.

**Momentum** is different from progress: it measures current connection to learning, over a 14-day window, with a floor of 20. It never drops to zero. Progress once earned is never lost.
