# 8. Impact and Progress

FARO gives points for **persisting**, not for clicking. Everything that awards points requires something real to have happened: an activity finished, a module closed, a return after disconnecting, three review cards completed, an answer that helped someone.

## How points are earned

| Action | Points | Exact rule |
|---|---|---|
| Complete an activity | **50** | Every completed activity on the route: submitted in Canvas or finished in FARO. |
| Close a module | **250** | When every activity in the module is completed. |
| Rhythm bonus | **+25** | Every 3 consecutive days with activity. A day without study only means it stops adding; it never subtracts. |
| Comeback bonus | **+125** | Once, when you return after 3 or more days without activity and complete something. |
| Review cards | **+75** | For finishing the three cards (not for getting them right first time). Once a day. |
| Answer marked helpful | **+25** | Another student marked your answer as helpful in Community. |
| Finish a study room | **+100** | Joining gives nothing. Finishing the full session does. |

The scale is built for a **semester**, not a course: finishing a whole course is worth 2,300–2,800 points, and a semester of about four courses reaches 10,000.

## The Impact screen

The tab is called **Impact** because it first shows what you did, and then what it is worth.

**1. Your effort.** Your **FARO Points this semester**: the big number adds up this course and the semester's previous courses. It counts up when you open the screen, and below it shows what you earned this week (*+250 this week*). Underneath, in words, what you did. *This semester you have:*

- *Completed N activities*
- *Finished M modules*
- *Come back after a break*
- *Reconnected with N review sets*
- *Studied N days this week*

Only the lines that apply appear: if you have not come back from a break, that line is not there.

**2. Your reward: $200 MXN** with the **TecmiRewards** tag. One track to 10,000 points, with marks at $50 and $100. It says how far is left and what finishing the current course is worth: *"Finishing Gestión de Proyectos is worth at least 1,450 more points."* The **Keep going** button takes you to the Journey: the reward always points back at the course.

**3. Semester rewards.** Three tiers:

| Reward | Points |
|---|---|
| $50 MXN | 2,500 |
| $100 MXN | 5,000 |
| $200 MXN | 10,000 |

Tiers are **not bought: they unlock**. Reaching 5,000 spends nothing. You decide when to redeem, and **redeeming closes the semester's reward at that tier**. Before you redeem a lower tier, FARO says it in those words: *"If you keep going, you can reach $200 MXN."* It is your decision, not a trap.

**4. Where they come from.** The breakdown for this course (activities, modules, rhythm, comeback, cards, community) and for the semester's previous courses.

**When you reach 10,000**, the card changes to **Reward unlocked!** with the button to redeem $200 MXN.

> **This is a prototype integration with TecmiRewards.** FARO counts the points; the equivalence, eligibility, limits and redemption belong to Tecmilenio's rules and systems, and FARO issues nothing. **The amounts, the semester's previous courses and redemption are simulated** (each simulated previous course carries 16 activities and 6 modules). The screen says so with a tag and in the footer. We found no public TecmiRewards API, so the real integration must be validated with Tecmilenio.

You can also ask the Mentor about your points: it tells you how many you are missing and, if you like, which concrete steps get you there (chapter 6).

## Resilience achievements

There are five. None is earned by opening the app.

| Achievement | Earned when |
|---|---|
| **The Comeback** | You return after 3 or more days without activity and complete an activity. |
| **Back on Track** | You have at least 2 recorded sessions and your momentum is back to 65% or more. |
| **Weathered the Storm** | You complete a session while having 2 or more overdue activities when you started it. |
| **Smart Session** | You complete a session that fitted in the time you declared you had. |
| **Finisher** | Your progress reaches 100%. |

While an achievement is not earned, FARO shows a **hint** of what it would take. Never a reproach.

## Community recognitions

| Recognition | Earned when |
|---|---|
| **Community Builder** | You take part in the course space consistently. |
| **Knowledge Sharer** | You share resources or notes that others use. |
| **Helpful Peer** | Your answers accumulate helpful marks. |
| **Study Companion** | You complete study rooms alongside others. |
| **Community Comeback** | You come back to the community after a period away. |

## Progress

The progress percentage is **not invented by FARO**: it comes from Canvas. It is activities with a recorded submission ÷ total course activities. An overdue date does not count as "done", and an activity that belongs to no module does not enter the count.

### Your rhythm

A card with the week, Monday to Sunday. A day counts as **active** if FARO recorded a session or Canvas recorded course activity that day. Today is ringed; days still to come are dashed. On top, *N active days this week*, and the rule: *"Flexible consistency, not perfection. A day off is not a broken streak."* There is no streak to lose.

### Momentum

**Momentum** is different from progress: it measures current connection to learning, over a 14-day window, with a floor of 20. It never drops to zero. Progress once earned is never lost.

Every time you earn points, a short notice appears at the top (*+50 points*) and fades on its own.
