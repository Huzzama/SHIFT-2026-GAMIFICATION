# 3. Home

Answers a single question: **where am I and what do I do now?** It is ordered top to bottom by what matters most.

## 1. Header

A greeting by time of day, the course name, the progress bar and the percentage. Tapping it opens Journey.

Top right, in the app bar, is your **momentum** with its percentage. It is always visible, on every screen.

## 2. Attention strip (only when it applies)

If FARO detected an interruption, a tappable strip appears:

- **Amber, "Welcome back"** — when you return after an absence. Opens Recovery.
- **White, with an attention message** — when your rhythm dropped but you did not leave. Opens the same.

If you are on track, nothing appears. FARO does not interrupt to congratulate you.

## 3. Next Best Step

The dark card. **One single activity**, never a list. It shows the name, the estimated minutes, the minutes you said you have, and a line explaining why it is that one.

**How it is chosen** (the full rule, deliberately boring):

1. Take the open activities: the missed ones, the current one and the upcoming ones.
2. Of those, the ones that **fit in your available time**.
3. Of those, **the shortest**.
4. If none fits, the shortest of all, and the text tells you to start it and pause whenever you want.

The reason changes by case. If the step is overdue: *"This step reopens the route. Everything after it gets easier."*

**Start this step** marks it as completed and moves everything at once: progress, momentum, journey, points and badges. **Ask FARO** opens the Mentor.

## 4. Community card

One line with how many people are studying your course right now. It only appears if there is someone. Opens Community.

## 5. The four pillars

**Focus** (your purpose) · **Advance** (the journey) · **Reward** (progress) · **Own** (the mentor). They are shortcuts, not new sections.

## 6. Your courses, momentum, rhythm and achievements

Tappable summary cards. **Momentum** carries a word instead of just the number: *strong momentum* (65% or more), *regaining momentum* (40–64%), *your progress is safe* (under 40%). That last one never says you are doing badly.

## Safe harbour

If you activated a planned pause from Recovery, Home is replaced by a single card saying your progress is exactly where you left it, with a button to continue whenever you want. No countdown, no reminders.
